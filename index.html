My Journey

How I Became a Doctor

Chapter 1 · Dubai

Born in Gold & Sunlight

The UAE raised me — its gleaming towers, its mix of cultures, its fearless energy. Growing up in Dubai made me comfortable everywhere and at home in the world. It's honestly the best foundation I could've asked for.

🇦🇪
Chapter 2 · Roots

An Indian Soul, Always

No matter how cosmopolitan I am, my Indian roots run deep. I love my festivals, my family's traditions, and that bond that only an Indian upbringing gives you. Both identities are mine, and I wear them together with pride.

🇮🇳
Chapter 3 · Medicine

I Chose to Heal

Medicine wasn't just a career choice — it was a calling I felt deep inside. I picked up my stethoscope, learned the language of the body, stood in wards at ungodly hours — and I'd do it all again. I currently work at NMC Royal Hospital in Dubai Silicon Oasis, and I'm still studying. Always learning.

🧪
Chapter 4 · Love

I Found My Person

Balancing the weight of medicine with the warmth of love — I found someone who makes it all feel worth it. I'm married, I'm loved, and I'm still wildly and unapologetically myself. That means everything to me.

💍
Chapter 5 · Now

Still Writing My Story

With my textbooks open and my dreams wide awake, I'm still on this journey. Every patient I meet, every exam I sit, every late night — it's all part of becoming the doctor I always knew I was meant to be.

🌟
